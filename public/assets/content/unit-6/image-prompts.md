## 🎨 Notes for Image Generation

1. **Consistency**: Maintain Ahmed and Sarah's appearance across all images
2. **Color Scheme**: Always include #FF6B35 (orange) and #FFB800 (gold) as accent colors
3. **Tech Aesthetic**: Futuristic but warm, not cold cyberpunk
4. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Sarah always wears hijab elegantly
5. **Progression**: Images should show Ahmed's growing confidence throughout
6. **Framework Visualizations**: Make abstract concepts visually concrete
7. **Emotional Arc**: From curiosity (start) → mastery (end)
8. **Quality**: All images should be photorealistic, 8K, cinematic quality
